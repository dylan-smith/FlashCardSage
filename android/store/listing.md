**Full description:**

FlashCardSage is a flash card app for learning Spanish. Add the words and phrases you want to learn, and it helps
with the rest.

- Type one side of a card and it fills in the translation.
- Give a card a picture of what it means, made for you or one of your own.
- Hear the Spanish read aloud, at normal speed or slowly.
- Practice your cards, or short sentences written from the words you're learning.
- Listening mode plays the Spanish before you see it.
- See a score for every card, so you know what to practice next.
- Keep practicing offline. Your answers are saved when you're back online.
- Organise cards with tags and decks, and start from built-in decks.
- Scan a document for new words, or get suggestions that go with your cards.

Sign in with your Google account. Your cards are yours alone, and the same account works at flashcardsage.com.

- **Account deletion URL:** https://flashcardsage.com/privacy#delete-account

## Data safety

Data is collected, isn't shared with third parties (Microsoft, Google and Cloudflare only process it to run the app),
is encrypted in transit, and users can ask for it to be deleted (the account deletion URL above).

| Data type | Collected | Why | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Account management, app functionality | No |
| Email address | Yes | Account management | No |
| User IDs | Yes | Account management, analytics | No |
| Photos | Yes (pictures the user puts on cards) | App functionality | Yes |
| Other user-generated content (cards, tags, decks) | Yes | App functionality | No |
| App interactions | Yes | Analytics | No |
| Crash logs and diagnostics | Yes | Analytics | No |

A document uploaded to scan for cards is only read to answer that request and isn't kept, so it counts as processed
ephemerally and isn't declared.
